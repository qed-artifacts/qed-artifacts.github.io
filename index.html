QED evokes quod erat demonstrandum—that which has been demonstrated—and names the path from quality evaluation to responsible disposition. In QED-AI, AI primarily means Artifacts Institute.

01

Public-benefit mission

Evaluation should lead somewhere responsible.

Our mission is to improve how artifacts, claims, systems, practices, situations, complex problems, decisions, and bodies of work are evaluated, matured, documented, contextualized, and responsibly carried forward.

QED-AI pursues that mission by developing a cross-domain quality framework, applying it through public-facing disposition dossiers and decision records, and producing or revising design-science artifacts when the evidence warrants an intervention.

We serve accountable stewards, evaluators, educators, researchers, leaders, practitioners, implementers, reviewers, decision-support actors, and affected communities. QED-AI supplies resources; it does not replace the authority, professional judgment, validation duties, or legal obligations of the people who use them.

Planned public-benefit work

Three streams. Four reusable artifact families.

These program areas are in formation and development. They are presented as planned work, not as claims of completed scale or impact.

01

Quality Framework R&D

Research and maintain concepts, rubrics, maturity structures, adequacy scales, vocabularies, schemas, evidence practices, validation logic, and documentation protocols for rigorous evaluation.

Planned outputs: open reference resources and evaluation tools.

02

Artifact Disposition Dossiers

Apply the framework to a situated problem, artifact ecology, program, system, or decision context and make the evidence, gaps, limitations, risks, and warranted disposition range reviewable.

Planned outputs: public, reusable dossier packages.

03

DSR Artifact Development

Create or revise methods, models, rubrics, workflows, templates, datasets, communication objects, and governance resources when a dossier or authorized decision identifies a justified need.

Planned outputs: documented and testable designed resources.

How knowledge becomes usable

A traceable operating pathway.

Selected initiatives move through a modular knowledge-operationalization pipeline. Each stage preserves provenance, domain meaning, limitations, authority, and reviewability.

  1. 01GroundQualify evidence and sources.
  2. 02NormalizeClarify terms and boundaries.
  3. 03OperationalizeDefine criteria, measures, and workflows.
  4. 04StructureMake the work computable and AI-legible.
  5. 05ApplyFit the artifact to people and context.
  6. 06EvaluateJudge adequacy and warranted disposition.
  7. 07CommunicateTranslate for the responsible audience.
  8. 08PreservePackage for review, reuse, and revision.
